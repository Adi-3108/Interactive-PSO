Welcome to SwarmLab!

SwarmLab helps you learn Particle Swarm Optimization interactively. Let's take a quick tour.

Choose a Problem

Pick a benchmark function from the left panel, or type your own expression.

Step 3 of 6

Tune Parameters

Adjust w, c₁, c₂, swarm size, and more. Watch how they affect convergence.

Watch the Swarm

The main canvas shows particles moving. Use transport controls to play, pause, or step through iterations.

Explore Modes

Try Hand-Calc Lab to work through PSO by hand, Predict mode to guess the next step, or Compare to benchmark different settings.
